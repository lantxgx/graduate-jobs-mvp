# SRC-20260913-dropbox-public-probe

- company: Dropbox
- candidate public board: `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/dropbox/jobs?content=true`
- candidate official careers URLs: `https://www.dropbox.com/jobs`, `https://jobs.dropbox.com/`
- state: deferred; not integrated

## Bounded probe

- The public board returned one internship-like posting: `Software Engineering Intern (Summer 2027)`.
- Both official careers URL probes were unavailable in the bounded check, so official ownership and current detail access could not be verified from the careers page.
- No source configuration or job data was changed.

## Decision

Defer Dropbox until its official recruitment page is reachable and directly confirms the public job route. Do not repeat the probe without new evidence.
